The elections to elect the members of the [[15th Puducherry Assembly]] was held on 6 April 2021. The elections were held for all the [[List of constituencies of the Puducherry Legislative Assembly|30 constituencies]] of the [[Puducherry Legislative Assembly]]. 

The [[National Democratic Alliance]] won a simple majority in the elections, and [[N. Rangaswamy]] of the [[All India N.R. Congress]] was sworn in as the [[Chief Minister of Puducherry|chief minister]] for the fourth time.

==Background==
Elections to a [[State legislative assemblies of India|legislative assembly]] in India are usually held once in five years, and the [[Member of the Legislative Assembly|members of the legislative assembly]] are directly elected to serve five year terms from [[Single-member district|single-member constituencies]]. The [[2016 Puducherry Legislative Assembly election|previous assembly elections]] were held in May 2021 to elect the 30 members of the [[14th Puducherry Assembly]].

In the previous election, the [[Indian National Congress]]-led [[United Progressive Alliance]] formed the [[Government of Puducherry|government]] after winning 17 of the 30 seats, and [[V. Narayanasamy]] sworn in as the [[Chief Minister of Puducherry|chief minister]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/elections-2016/puducherry-elections-2016/news/congress-emerges-as-the-single-largest-party-with-15-seats-in-30-member-puducherry-assembly/articleshow/52343485.cms|title=Congress emerges as the single largest party in Puducherry|work=[[The Times of India]]|date=20 May 2016|access-date=20 March 2021|archive-date=28 September 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210928042522/https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/elections-2016/puducherry-elections-2016/news/congress-emerges-as-the-single-largest-party-with-15-seats-in-30-member-puducherry-assembly/articleshow/52343485.cms|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url=https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/elections-2016/puducherry-elections-2016/news/congress-dmk-front-regains-power-in-pondy/articleshow/52355802.cms|title=Congress-DMK front regains power in Pondy|work=[[The Times of India]]|date=20 May 2016|access-date=20 March 2021|archive-date=28 September 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210928053200/https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/elections-2016/puducherry-elections-2016/news/congress-dmk-front-regains-power-in-pondy/articleshow/52355802.cms|url-status=live}}</ref> [[All India N.R. Congress]] (AINRC), which had formed the government under [[N. Rangaswamy]] after the [[2011 Puducherry Legislative Assembly election|2011 elections]], won eight seats, and Rangaswamy became the [[List of leaders of the opposition in the Puducherry Legislative Assembly|leader of the opposition]] in the assembly.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.thehindu.com/elections/puducherry2016/Live-Puducherry-Assembly-election-results-2016/article60512135.ece|title=Puducherry CM Rangasamy resigns|date=6 June 2016|access-date=1 June 2025|work=[[The Hindu]]}}</ref>

In February 2021, a [[trust vote]] was called by the [[Lieutenant Governor of Puducherry|lieutenant governor]] after five members of the Congress including two ministers, and one member of the [[Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam]] (DMK) resigned from the assembly.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/puducherry-congress-v-narayanasamy-confidence-motion-kiran-bedi-7198641/|title=Explained: The trust vote in Puducherry|date=21 February 2021|access-date=1 June 2025|work=[[The Indian Express]]}}</ref> With the strength of the house reduced to 27, which included the three nominated members, the government had the support of only 11 members. After the government lost the trust vote, Narayanasamy resigned as chief minister on 22 February. Later, Narayanasamy stated that the government lost because the speaker accepted the votes from nominated members on equal standing to the elected ones, and the lieutenant governor, who was a former member of the [[Bharatiya Janata Party]] (BJP), had appointed members of the same party for all the nominated seats.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/national/congress-loses-puducherry-narayanasamy-blames-bjp/article33901435.ece|work=[[Business Line]]|date=22 February 2021|title=Congress loses Puducherry|access-date=13 May 2023|archive-date=13 May 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230513204654/https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/national/congress-loses-puducherry-narayanasamy-blames-bjp/article33901435.ece|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Government formation==
{{see|Fourth Rangaswamy ministry}}

As the NDA achieved a majority in the elections, the AINRC&ndash;BJP combine formed the next government.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.indiatoday.in/elections/puducherry-assembly-polls-2021/story/puducherry-election-results-2021-full-list-of-winners-1798043-2021-05-02|title=Puducherry Election Results 2021: NDA wins in Pondy by bagging 16 seats, here's the list of winners|work=[[India Today]]|date=3 May 2021|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref> The lieutenant governor appointed three members to the assembly, all of whom belonged to the BJP, raising its count to nine in the assembly.<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/tamil-nadu/2021/may/11/three-nominated-mlas-take-bjps-tally-in-puducherry-to-9-2301057.html |work=[[The New Indian Express]] |date=11 May 2021 |title=Three nominated MLAs take BJP's tally to nine  |access-date=13 May 2023 |archive-date=13 May 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230513205708/https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/tamil-nadu/2021/may/11/three-nominated-mlas-take-bjps-tally-in-puducherry-to-9-2301057.html |url-status=live }}</ref> Furthermore, the six independent members pledged support to the government, increasing its strength to 25 in the 33-member assembly. Rangaswamy was sworn in as the chief minister of Puducherry for the fourth time on 7 May 2021.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/puducherry/ainrcs-rangasamy-sworn-in-as-puducherry-chief-minister-for-fourth-time/article34506931.ece|title=AINRC’s Rangasamy sworn-in as Puducherry Chief Minister for fourth time|date=7 May 2021|access-date=1 June 2025|work=[[The Hindu]]}}</ref> On 8 June, the alliance announced that the BJP would get two ministers and the post of the assembly speaker, while AIRNC got three ministers.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/puducherry/bjp-keeps-rangasamy-on-tenterhooks-in-puducherry/articleshow/95607301.cms|title=BJP keeps Rangasamy on tenterhooks in Puducherry|date=18 November 2022|access-date=1 June 2025|work=[[The Times of India]]}}</ref>
