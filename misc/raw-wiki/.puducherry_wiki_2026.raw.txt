Legislative Assembly elections are expected to be held in [[Puducherry (union territory)|Puducherry]] before 15 June 2026 to elect [[List of constituencies of the Puducherry Legislative Assembly|30 members]] of the [[Puducherry Legislative Assembly]].

== Background ==
Elections to a [[State legislative assemblies of India|Legislative Assembly]] in India are usually held once in five years, and the [[Member of the Legislative Assembly|members of the legislative assembly]] are directly elected to serve five year terms from [[Single-member district|single-member constituencies]]. The tenure of [[Puducherry Legislative Assembly]] is scheduled to end on 15 June 2026.<ref>{{cite web |title=Term of the Houses |url=https://www.eci.gov.in/term-of-the-houses |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250807185225/https://www.eci.gov.in/term-of-the-houses |archive-date=7 August 2025 |access-date=1 June 2025|work=[[Election Commission of India]] |url-status=live }}</ref> In the [[2021 Puducherry Legislative Assembly election|previous assembly elections]] held in April 2021, the [[All India N.R. Congress]]-led [[National Democratic Alliance]] formed the [[Government of Puducherry|state government]] after winning 16 out of 30 seats in the assembly, with [[N. Rangaswamy]] sworn in as the [[Chief Minister of Puducherry|Chief Minister]].<ref>{{cite news |date=3 May 2021 |title=NR Congress-BJP alliance emerges victorious in Puducherry assembly polls |url=https://www.business-standard.com/article/elections/nr-congress-bjp-alliance-emerges-victorious-in-puducherry-assembly-polls-121050300106_1.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221206233900/https://www.business-standard.com/article/elections/nr-congress-bjp-alliance-emerges-victorious-in-puducherry-assembly-polls-121050300106_1.html |archive-date=6 December 2022 |work=[[Business Standard]] |access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref> In the later part of 2025, the [[Election Commission of India]] conducted a [[Special Intensive Revision]] after 24 years, to revise the electoral rolls.<ref>{{cite news |date=29 October 2025 |title=SIR rollout schedule announced in Puducherry |url=https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/puducherry/sir-rollout-schedule-announced-in-puducherry/article70215905.ece |access-date=2 November 2025 |work=[[The Hindu]] |language=en-IN |issn=0971-751X}}</ref>
