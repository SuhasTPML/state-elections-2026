Legislative Assembly elections are expected to be held in [[Elections in Tamil Nadu|Tamil Nadu]] before 10 May 2026 to elect all [[List of constituencies of the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly|234 members]] of the [[Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly]].

== Background ==
Elections to a [[State legislative assemblies of India|State Legislative Assembly]] in India are usually held once in five years, and the [[Member of the Legislative Assembly|members of the legislative assembly]] are directly elected to serve five year terms from [[Single-member district|single-member constituencies]]. The [[2021 Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly election|previous assembly elections]] were held in April 2021 to elect the 234 members of the [[16th Tamil Nadu Assembly]], and the tenure of the [[Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly|assembly]] ends on 10 May 2026.<ref>{{cite web |title=Term of the Houses |url=https://www.eci.gov.in/term-of-the-houses |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250807185225/https://www.eci.gov.in/term-of-the-houses |archive-date=7 August 2025 |access-date=13 October 2025 |work=[[Election Commission of India]] }}</ref> In the previous election, the [[Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam]] (DMK)-led [[Secular Progressive Alliance]] (SPA) formed the [[Government of Tamil Nadu|state government]] after winning 159 of the 234 seats, and [[M. K. Stalin]] sworn in as the [[Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu|chief minister]].<ref>{{cite news |date=5 May 2021 |title=Assembly elections 2021: DMK reclaims power in Tamil Nadu |url=https://www.thehindu.com/data/data-assembly-elections-2021-dmk-reclaims-power-in-tamil-nadu/article34490348.ece |access-date=1 June 2025 |work=[[The Hindu]] |language=en-IN |issn=0971-751X}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |date=7 May 2021 |title=DMK chief MK Stalin takes oath as Tamil Nadu Chief Minister |url=https://www.indiatoday.in/india/tamil-nadu/story/dmk-chief-mk-stalin-oath-tamil-nadu-chief-minister-cabinet-1799754-2021-05-07 |access-date=30 October 2025 |work=[[India Today]]}}</ref> The [[All India Anna Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam]] (AIADMK), which won 66 seats, became the principal opposition party and its leader [[Edappadi K. Palaniswami]] was elected and served as the [[List of leaders of the opposition in the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly|leader of the opposition]].<ref>{{cite news|date=10 May 2021|title=Edappadi Palaniswami elected as Leader of Opposition in Tamil Nadu Assembly|url=https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/tamil-nadu/2021/may/10/edappadi-palaniswami-elected-as-leader-of-opposition-in-tamil-nadu-assembly-2300755.html|work=[[The Indian Express]]|access-date=11 June 2021|archive-date=13 May 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210513143613/https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/tamil-nadu/2021/may/10/edappadi-palaniswami-elected-as-leader-of-opposition-in-tamil-nadu-assembly-2300755.html|url-status=live}}</ref>

In June 2022, three members&ndash;[[O. Panneerselvam]], [[P. H. Manoj Pandian]], and [[R. Vaithilingam]] were expelled from the AIADMK.<ref>{{cite news |title=AIADMK Expels OPS, His Supporters From Party; Defiant Leader Vows Action |url=https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/aiadmk-expels-ops-his-supporters-from-party-defiant-leader-vows-action-3146536 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220712181227/https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/aiadmk-expels-ops-his-supporters-from-party-defiant-leader-vows-action-3146536 |archive-date=12 July 2022 |access-date=7 November 2025 |work=[[NDTV]]}}</ref> In August 2022, [[P. Ayyappan]] of the AIADMK also joined the expelled faction.<ref>{{cite news |date=27 August 2022 |title=Usilampatti MLA switches over to Panneerselvam camp |url=https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/usilampatti-mla-switches-over-to-panneerselvam-camp/article65819191.ece |access-date=7 November 2025 |work=[[The Hindu]] |language=en-IN |issn=0971-751X}}</ref> [[K. A. Sengottaiyan]] was expelled from AIADMK in October 2025,<ref>{{cite news |last=Jesudasan |first=Dennis S. |date=31 October 2025 |title=Tamil Nadu MLA Sengottaiyan expelled from AIADMK a day after his meeting with Panneerselvam, Dhinakaran, Sasikala |url=https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/tn-mla-ka-sengottaiyan-expelled-from-aiadmk-a-day-after-meeting-ops-dhinakaran-sasikala/article70225526.ece |access-date=7 November 2025 |work=[[The Hindu]] |language=en-IN |issn=0971-751X}}</ref> and subsequently resigned from his position as a member of the assembly in November 2025.<ref>{{cite news |date=27 November 2025 |title=Expelled AIADMK veteran Sengottaiyan joins Vijay's TVK |url=https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/ka-sengottaiyan-former-tn-minister-expelled-from-aiadmk-joins-tvk/article70329096.ece |access-date=7 December 2025 |work=[[The Hindu]] |language=en-IN |issn=0971-751X}}</ref> Manoj Pandian resigned as a member of the assembly in November 2025 and joined the DMK,<ref>{{cite news |date=5 November 2025 |title=AIADMK MLA Manoj Pandian joins DMK, resigns as MLA |url=https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chennai/aiadmk-mla-manoj-pandian-joins-dmk-resigns-as-mla/articleshow/125095599.cms |access-date=1 June 2025 |work=[[The Times of India]] |issn=0971-8257}}</ref> with Vaithilingam following suit in January 2026.<ref>{{cite news |date=21 January 2026 |title=Former AIADMK Minister R. Vaithilingam joins DMK |url=https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/r-vaithilingam-resigns-aiadmk-dmk-january-21-2026/article70532054.ece |access-date=22 January 2026 |work=[[The Hindu]]}}</ref> In late 2025, the [[Pattali Makkal Katchi]] (PMK) split into two factions, with three members expressing support to [[Anbumani Ramadoss]] and two members supporting [[S. Ramadoss faction)|splinter faction]] of the PMK.<ref>{{cite news |date=16 September 2025 |title=EC recognises Anbumani as PMK president; Ramadoss camp calls it a unilateral decision |url=https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chennai/ec-recognises-anbumani-as-pmk-president-ramadoss-camp-calls-it-a-unilateral-decision/articleshow/123908124.cms |access-date=7 November 2025 |work=[[The Times of India]] |issn=0971-8257}}</ref> Two members&ndash;[[T. K. Amulkandasami]] (AIADMK) and [[K. Ponnusamy (DMK politician)|K. ==Voter statistics==
According to the [[Election Commission of India]], 56,707,380 voters were eligible to vote in the assembly elections in Tamil Nadu. This includes 27,738,925 male, 28,960,838 female, and 7,617 [[third gender]] voters.<ref name="Vote"/>

=== AIADMK-led Alliance ===

== Campaign ==
On 1 June 2025, DMK president and chief minister [[M. K. Stalin]] launched 'Oraniyil Tamil Nadu' (Tamil Nadu as one team), an enrollment drive to add new party members and urged the party cadres to enroll at least 30% of voters in each polling booth to the party through a door-to-door outreach.<ref>{{cite news |date=2 June 2025 |title=DMK launched ‘Oraniyil Tamil Nadu’ enrolment drive |url=https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/madurai/dmk-launched-oraniyil-tamil-nadu-enrolment-drive/articleshow/121555410.cms |access-date=1 January 2026 |work=[[The Times of India]] |issn=0971-8257}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |date=2 July 2025 |title=CM Stalin launches Oraniyil Tamil Nadu, does not rule out more parties joining DMK bloc |url=https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/tamil-nadu/2025/Jul/02/cm-stalin-launches-oraniyil-tamil-nadu-does-not-rule-out-more-parties-joining-dmk-bloc |access-date=1 January 2026 |work=[[The New Indian Express]] }}</ref>

On 7 July 2025, AIADMK general secretary Palaniswami launched a statewide campaign with the slogan 'Let's Protect the People, Let's Save Tamil Nadu'.<ref>{{cite news |agency=ANI |date=5 July 2025 |title=EPS launches AIADMK campaign logo for 2026 TN polls, gives out call to ‘remove evil DMK govt’ |url=https://theprint.in/india/eps-launches-aiadmk-campaign-logo-for-2026-tn-polls-gives-out-call-to-remove-evil-dmk-govt/2680644/ |access-date=8 November 2025 |work=[[ThePrint]] }}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=AIADMK General Secretary Edappadi K. Palaniswami launches 'Let's Protect the People, Let's Save Tamil Nadu' ahead of Tamil Nadu Assembly election |url=https://www.aninews.in/news/national/general-news/aiadmk-general-secretary-edappadi-k-palaniswami-launches-lets-protect-the-people-lets-save-tamil-nadu-ahead-of-tamil-nadu-assembly-election20250707112508/ |access-date=8 November 2025 |work=[[ANI News]] }}</ref>

On 13 September 2025, TVK president Vijay launched his party's election campaign in [[Tiruchirappalli]], with subsequent rallies planned on weekends.<ref>{{cite news |date=14 September 2025 |title=‘Vijay is coming’: Tamil star kicks off Assembly poll campaign, targets DMK and BJP over broken promises |url=https://indianexpress.com/article/political-pulse/vijay-kicks-off-campaign-in-trichy-blasts-dmk-and-bjp-over-broken-promises-10248461/ |access-date=8 November 2025 |work=[[The Indian Express]]}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |date=13 September 2025 |title=Vijay launches election campaign in Trichy, says TVK will make only practical and implementable promises |url=https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/trichy/vijay-launches-election-campaign-in-trichy-says-tvk-will-make-only-practical-and-implementable-promises/articleshow/123869217.cms |access-date=8 November 2025 |work=[[The Times of India]] |issn=0971-8257}}</ref> However, following a [[2025 Karur crowd crush|crowd crush]] that resulted in the deaths of 41 people and injuries to 80-120 others, during a political rally hosted by he TVK in [[Karur]], he suspended the campaign temporarily.<ref>{{cite news |date=28 September 2025 |title=39 die in stampede during TVK leader Vijay's Karur campaign |url=https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chennai/10-die-in-stampede-like-situation-during-tvk-leader-vijays-karur-campaign/articleshow/124183777.cms |access-date=30 October 2025 |work=[[The Times of India]] |issn=0971-8257}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |date=27 September 2025 |title=TVK rally stampede highlights: Madurai Bench of Madras High Court to hear TVK's case on September 29 |url=https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/tvk-vijay-rally-karur-updates-on-september-27-2025/article70102676.ece |access-date=30 October 2025 |work=[[The Hindu]] }}</ref>

Following an order of the [[Madras High Court]],<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.ndtv.com/chennai-news/no-one-above-law-court-orders-making-rules-for-rallies-by-political-parties-9302892 |title=Court Orders Making Rules For Rallies By Political Parties |work=[[NDTV]] |date=18 September 2025 |access-date=30 October 2025}}</ref> the [[Government of Tamil Nadu]] announced new rules and guidelines for political rallies in the state in January 2026.<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/tamil-nadu-government-notifies-sop-for-political-meetings-rallies-other-large-public-gatherings/article70479181.ece |title=Tamil Nadu government notifies SOP for political meetings, rallies, other large public gatherings |work=[[The Hindu]] |date=7 January 2026 |access-date=10 January 2026}}</ref>
