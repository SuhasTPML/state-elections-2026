The legislative assembly election in the Indian union territory of [[Puducherry (union territory)|Puducherry]] was held on 16 May 2016 to elect members of the 30 constituencies in the non-contiguous territory to constitute the [[Fourteenth Assembly of Puducherry|Fourteenth Puducherry Legislative Assembly]].<ref name="th"/>

== Background ==

It is a legal requirement that the election must be held at an interval of five years, or earlier if the assembly is dissolved by the [[President of India|President]]. The [[2011 Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly election|previous election]] was conducted on 13 April 2011 and its term would have naturally expired on 2 June 2016. The election was organised and conducted by the [[Election Commission of India]] (ECI).

The [[Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam]] and the [[Indian National Congress]] were in an alliance and contested 9 and 21 seats respectively. Ruling AINRC, BJP & ADMK will contest separately.

The polling booths in three Assembly constituencies in Puducherry (out of total 30 segments) were equipped with [[Voter-verified paper audit trail]] (VVPAT) machines along with EVMs.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://eci.nic.in/eci_main1/current/PN16_04032016.pdf|title=VVPAT usage in 64 seats in 5 states Schedule for the General Elections to the Legislative Assemblies of Assam, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal and Puducherry.}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/puducherry/ec-holds-first-phase-of-training-programme/article8319611.ece|title=EC holds first phase of training programme|author=Staff Reporter|date=6 March 2016|via=www.thehindu.com}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/around-20-000-vvpat-machines-to-be-used-in-assly-polls-cec-116021001047_1.html|title=Around 20,000 VVPAT machines to be used in assly polls: CEC|agency=Press Trust of India|date=10 February 2016|via=Business Standard}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/puducherry/cec-asks-poll-officials-to-remain-neutral/article8221669.ece|title=CEC asks poll officials to remain neutral|first=R.|last=Sivaraman|date=11 February 2016|via=www.thehindu.com}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.deccanchronicle.com/nation/politics/110216/parties-seek-single-phase-polls.html|title=Parties seek single phase polls|date=11 February 2016|website=Deccan Chronicle}}</ref>

== Voting ==
The election was held on 16 May 2016 in a single phase and 84.11% voting was recorded.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.thehindu.com/elections/puducherry2016/Brisk-voting-in-Union-Territory/article14324525.ece|title=Brisk voting in Union Territory|first=S.|last=Prasad|date=17 May 2016|via=www.thehindu.com}}</ref> The votes were counted on 19 May and results were declared on same day.<ref name="th">{{cite news|url=http://www.thehindu.com/news/national/election-dates-for-five-states-announced/article8313813.ece |title=4 States, Puducherry to go to polls between April 4 and May 16 |work=The Hindu |date=4 March 2016}}</ref>
